EMBERWARD

Raise a settlement in the last warm valley. Build by daylight. Stand with your people when the blight arrives.

Mouse to build · R or right-click rotates · Space pauses · 1–9 select tools

FIELD MANUAL

Keep the fire alive

  1. Assign. Set builders, gatherers, and farmers. Unassigned settlers remain idle until you give them a job.
  2. Gather. Mark trees, stone, and berry bushes. Gatherers physically carry every bundle back to the Heartfire stockpile.
  3. Plan. Buildings have different footprints. Press R or right-click while placing to rotate them into tight spaces.
  4. Build. A blueprint spends nothing immediately. Builders carry wood and stone to it first, then construct it.
  5. Live. Settlers stop to eat and drink. Farmers tend fields and carry each harvest home instead of creating passive income.
  6. Defend. The blight attacks at night. Use the terrain and compact defensive courtyards to buy time.
  7. Intervene. Spend Spirit on guardian powers when the colony cannot cope alone. Cast Ward on each violet shrine to cleanse it.
